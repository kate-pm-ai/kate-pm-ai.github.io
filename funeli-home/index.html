What is a web2app funnel

Sell the subscription on the web, deliver it in the app

A web2app funnel takes a user from an ad to a web quiz, a personalised paywall and a web checkout, and only then sends them to the App Store or Google Play – already a paying subscriber. Funeli is where you build, publish and measure these funnels.

FAQ

Questions

What is a web2app funnel?

A web2app funnel moves a user from an ad to a web quiz, a paywall and a web checkout, and then to the app in the App Store or Google Play. The subscription is sold on the web before the install.

Who is Funeli for?

Paid user acquisition teams with subscription apps in mental health, sleep, microlearning, language learning, fitness and health, buying traffic on Meta, TikTok and similar channels.

Which payment provider does Funeli use?

Paddle. Payments are set up per workspace and support configurable paywalls, wallet-first checkout and upsell checkout sheets.

Can I publish funnels on my own domain?

Yes. Connect a custom domain to your workspace and Funeli uses it across all published funnels.

Can AI build the funnel for me?

Yes. Describe the funnel to the built-in agent or to ChatGPT inside Funeli: it drafts the screens, checks the structure and edits on request. External agents can connect through MCP.
